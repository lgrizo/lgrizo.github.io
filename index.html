APP INFORMATION

PNG Vault Manager

A private file-management utility for its authorized operator.

Purpose and permissions

The app helps its operator organize digital design files in Google Drive. It requests the drive.file permission to create and manage only files created by the app or explicitly selected for use with it.

The app is operated privately and does not offer public sign-up. These pages provide the app and data-use information required for Google authorization.

Control and support

Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. For support or privacy requests, contact the app operator using the support address shown on Google's authorization screen.
